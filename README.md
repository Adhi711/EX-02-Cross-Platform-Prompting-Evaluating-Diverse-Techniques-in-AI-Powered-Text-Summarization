## EX-02: Cross-Platform Prompting – Evaluating Diverse Techniques in AI-Powered Text Summarization
## NAME: ADHARSH VIDYARDH U
## REG NO:212224230007
### AIM

To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of different prompting techniques such as **zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, and role-based prompting** across different AI platforms such as **ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot** for the task of **text summarization**.

### SCENARIO

A 500-word technical article on **“The Basics of Blockchain Technology”** is given to different AI platforms. The same article is summarized using different prompting techniques. The generated summaries are then compared based on **accuracy, coherence, simplicity, speed, and user experience**.

---

## ALGORITHM

**Step 1:** Select a technical article of approximately 500 words on **“The Basics of Blockchain Technology.”**

**Step 2:** Select AI platforms such as **ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot** for the experiment.

**Step 3:** Apply **Zero-Shot Prompting** by directly asking each AI platform to summarize the article without providing examples.

**Step 4:** Apply **Few-Shot Prompting** by providing one or two examples of summaries before asking the AI to summarize the given article.

**Step 5:** Apply **Chain-of-Thought Prompting** by instructing the AI to first identify the important concepts and then produce a concise summary. For evaluation, only the final summary is considered.

**Step 6:** Apply **Role-Based Prompting** by assigning a role such as *“You are an expert educational content summarizer”* and asking the AI to create a student-friendly summary.

**Step 7:** Record the summaries generated by each platform and prompting technique.

**Step 8:** Evaluate each summary based on **accuracy, coherence, simplicity, speed, and user experience**.

**Step 9:** Compare the results obtained from different combinations of AI platforms and prompting techniques.

**Step 10:** Identify the combination that produces the most suitable summary according to the evaluation criteria.

---

## PROMPTS USED

### 1. Zero-Shot Prompt

> Summarize the following 500-word article on “The Basics of Blockchain Technology” in simple English for undergraduate students. Keep the summary concise and include the important concepts.

### 2. Few-Shot Prompt

> Example 1: Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger that records transactions securely across multiple computers.
> Summary: Blockchain is a secure and decentralized system for recording transactions.
>
> Now summarize the given article on “The Basics of Blockchain Technology” in a similar simple and concise style.

### 3. Chain-of-Thought Prompt

> Analyze the article by identifying its main concepts, important terms, and key ideas. Then use these points to create a clear and concise summary of “The Basics of Blockchain Technology” for undergraduate students.

### 4. Role-Based Prompt

> You are an expert educational content summarizer. Summarize the following article on “The Basics of Blockchain Technology” for undergraduate students. Use simple language, maintain technical accuracy, and include only the most important concepts.

| Criteria            | Description                                                                              |

| **Coherence**       | Checks whether the ideas are logically connected and easy to follow.                     |

| **Speed**           | Measures the approximate time taken by the AI platform to generate the summary.          |
| **User Experience** | Considers readability, clarity, relevance, and ease of interaction.                      |

## RESULT

The experiment demonstrates that **prompting technique can affect the quality, clarity, and relevance of AI-generated summaries across different AI platforms**. Zero-shot prompting provides a direct baseline, while few-shot prompting provides examples that can guide the output style. Role-based prompting can help produce summaries suited to a particular audience, while structured reasoning instructions can help organize important concepts. The results can be compared using accuracy, coherence, simplicity, speed, and user experience to understand the differences between prompting approaches and platforms.
